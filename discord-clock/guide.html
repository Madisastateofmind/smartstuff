A free world clock to help friends compare times across time zones.

Set up and launch the clock

  1. Open World Clock Together’s Discord app listing and follow the prompts to add the app. If you are adding it to a server, choose the server where you want to use the clock and complete Discord’s prompts.

    If the link opens Discord in your browser, sign in there to add the app. Then return to the Discord desktop or mobile app to launch it.

  2. Open the text channel where you want people to find and launch the clock. Create a dedicated channel such as world-clock, or choose an existing one. Launch it here, rather than in a separate bot-testing channel. A voice call is not required.
  3. Open Discord’s App Launcher in the channel, find World Clock Together, and choose Launch. If Discord offers the app’s Launch command while you type /, you can select that instead.
  4. Discord opens the live clock in its Activity view. Use the timezone dropdown to compare times with your local time. When you browse elsewhere, Discord may show the clock in a floating window.
  5. Discord posts an Activity invitation card in the channel you launched from. Friends can use that card to join. Pin the card if you want to make it easier to find.

Add the app once; launch it in your chosen channel. Discord installs apps to a server or your account, rather than to an individual channel. Launching WCT from your chosen channel puts its invitation card there. To put a card in another channel, launch WCT from that channel too.

The dropdown uses fixed UTC offsets. Those selections do not automatically adjust for daylight saving time. The named London, Eastern and Pacific clocks adjust using your browser’s time-zone data.

Open it again later

The app remains installed when an Activity session ends. Launch it again through the App Launcher, or use Play on its invitation card if Discord offers it. You do not need to install the app again.

Keep the clock visible on desktop

After launching the clock, use Discord’s Pop Out option in the Activity view to keep it in a separate floating window while you browse other channels. Resize the window to suit you; the clock automatically adjusts to the available space.

If Discord shows a Reload Activity confirmation when you pop it out, choose Reload to continue. Reloading may reset your selected UTC offset and view size.

Use the clock’s − and + buttons to adjust its content size from 10% to 500%. 100% is the automatic fit, and Reset returns to it. Enlarged views may need scrolling.

Desktop tip: This pop-out option has been tested on computer. Mobile availability and behaviour are still being checked.

The floating window keeps an open Activity handy; it does not make the clock a permanently running participant in a channel. If the session ends, launch the clock again.

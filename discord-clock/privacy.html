Privacy Policy

World Time Together · Madworld
Last updated: October 10, 2026

About this policy

Madworld, maintained by GitHub user Madisastateofmind, operates World Time Together, a free world clock website and Discord Activity. This policy describes the current version.

Information used by the clock

The clock reads your device's current time and browser-provided time zone to display local and world times. Your dropdown selection is processed in your browser. The clock does not request precise location or send your local time zone or dropdown selection to an operator-controlled server.

Discord integration

When opened inside Discord, the app uses Discord's Embedded App SDK to establish its Activity connection. Discord supplies launch and session context, which may include identifiers for the Activity instance, server or channel. The current app does not request Discord account authorization, read messages, access microphone audio, or store Discord profile information. Launch context is not deliberately logged or retained by the app operator.

Cookies, analytics and retention

The current app adds no analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or persistent browser storage. The operator does not maintain a user database, sell personal data, or retain clock selections after the page closes. This does not cover technical information processed by hosting and platform providers.

Hosting and third-party services

The website and audio files are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. When accessed through Discord, requests may pass through Discord's proxy, and Discord processes platform and usage information under its own policy. See GitHub's Privacy Statement and Discord's Privacy Policy for their practices, retention and rights.

A separately installed voice bot, such as Noxyr, is an independent service. It is not part of this Activity and is governed by that provider's policies.

Your choices and questions

You can stop the clock by closing the website or Activity. For questions or privacy requests, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile or the repository's issue tracker. Public issues are visible to others: do not post personal or sensitive information. You can ask for a private contact method there. Requests concerning data held by GitHub or Discord should also be directed to those providers.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The update date will appear at the top of this page.
